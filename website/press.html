THE SHORT DESCRIPTION

For a story, listing or introduction.

CharmBits is a little companion for your workday. Hang a character above your desktop apps or inside VS Code, focus on one thing, celebrate a small win, and take a playful break. The VS Code extension and its features are free, with optional paid charms. Developed in Silicon Valley and made in California.
