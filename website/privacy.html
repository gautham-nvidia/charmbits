Your download request

We ask only for your email to send a confirmation link through Brevo. No username or password is created. Cloudflare provides the website, verification service, protected file storage and a check against automated signup abuse.

Confirmation links expire after 15 minutes and work once. The address in a pending request is cleared when the link is used; expired requests are removed by scheduled cleanup. A short download session lasts up to one hour. Its random access token is kept in this browser tab's session storage so you can return from checkout.

Trials and purchases

The service retains a keyed, one-way identifier of your normalized email to remember your original trial deadline and your purchases. Reinstalling or requesting another email link does not reset the trial. Gmail dots and plus aliases count as the same address. There is no payment card or automatic charge for a trial.

When checkout opens, Lemon Squeezy will process payment, receipts and applicable transaction taxes. It receives the billing details you enter there; CharmBits does not receive your full card number. We keep purchase references, item ownership and refund status so your purchases can be restored.

Offers are optional

You can join through the Pro offers popup, or choose the separate, unchecked offers box when requesting a download. Either route asks you to confirm your email before joining the list. Requesting a download alone does not opt you into offers. Subscribing does not start your trial or unlock Pro. Offer emails will include an unsubscribe link, and you can keep using the app after unsubscribing.

Your photos and app

Photos you try in the website locket stay in the current browser tab. They are not uploaded or saved by this page. The site stores your colour-theme choice, basket and the time you dismissed the offers popup on this device. Dismissal pauses automatic offers for seven days; you can reopen them from the footer. Your email is not saved in browser storage. Your installed CharmBits settings, photos and progress stay in the app's local storage. The editor extension includes every released charm and feature without an account. Optional desktop purchases and the desktop trial use verified email.

An activated standalone desktop app checks signed access with the service when connected. It sends the signed access record, not your photos, code or garden history. The free editor extension does not poll the purchase service. Purchase access files are personal; keep them out of public repositories.

Optional Nimbus weather

Weather is off until you enable it in the installed app. You choose a US airport observation station; CharmBits does not request GPS access. The app saves your station and temperature-unit preferences locally and checks the US National Weather Service about every ten minutes while the app is visible.

The weather request sends the selected station identifier to api.weather.gov. The service also receives the ordinary connection IP address. CharmBits does not send your code, photos, email, garden or focus history with weather requests. Turning weather off or hiding CharmBits stops those requests. Readings show their observation time; unavailable or older data is labelled. See the National Weather Service privacy policy ↗.

Our email sender and provider accounts must be connected before real signup starts. Trial identifiers are retained to enforce one trial per email; purchase records are retained to honour your access.
